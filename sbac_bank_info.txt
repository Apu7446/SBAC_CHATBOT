SECTION 4: COMPLETE LIST OF DEPOSIT & SAVINGS ACCOUNTS
================================================================================

A. DEMAND & SAVINGS ACCOUNTS:
1. SBAC Savings Deposit Account: Standard interest-bearing savings account for individuals.
2. SBAC Current Deposit Account: Non-interest bearing account designed for businesses, traders, and high-frequency transactions with cheque book facilities.
3. SBAC Short Notice Deposit Account (SND): Interest-bearing account where funds can be withdrawn upon giving short notice (usually 7 to 30 days). Ideal for corporate/institutional idle funds.
4. SBAC Samriddhi: Specialized high-yield deposit account for non-individual and institutional entities with slab-based attractive interest.
5. SBAC Student Savings Account: Low/zero-fee savings account for school, college, and university students.
6. SBAC Parua: Dedicated savings scheme for school-going children and young students with a minimum balance requirement of only Tk 500.
7. SBAC Ucchasha: Specialized higher education savings account designed for college/university students with a minimum balance requirement of Tk 1,000.
8. SBAC Senior Citizen Savings Account: Specially designed for senior citizens (aged 50/60+) offering preferential higher interest rates and dedicated service.
9. SBAC Probashi: Savings account for Non-Resident Bangladeshis (NRBs) and wage earners abroad with simple remittance facilities (Min balance Tk 1,000).
10. SBAC Payroll Account: Corporate salary accounts with zero/low minimum balance, free digital banking, and instant salary disbursement.
11. SBAC Surokkha Savings Account: Savings account bundled with security/insurance benefits for account holders.
12. SBAC Fast Account: Paperless digital account opening via e-KYC (https://sbacdigital.com) in minutes from anywhere using NID.
13. Financial Inclusion Accounts:
    - Tk 10 Privileged Savings Account (Farmers / Unbanked individuals)
    - Tk 50 Privileged Savings Account (Garment & Industrial workers)
    - Tk 100 Student Savings Account (School students)

B. TERM DEPOSIT & DPS SCHEMES (DEPOSIT PENSION SCHEMES):
1. Fixed Deposit Receipt (FDR): Term deposits for 1, 3, 6, 12, 24, 36 months offering fixed guaranteed return.
2. Monthly Savings Scheme (MSS / DPS): Monthly installment savings scheme for 1 to 10 years for building wealth.
3. Double Benefit Scheme (DBS): Scheme where deposited principal doubles over a fixed tenure.
4. Triple Benefit Scheme (TBS): Scheme where deposited principal triples over a fixed tenure.
5. Monthly Benefit / Income Scheme (MBS / MIS): Deposit lump-sum amount and receive monthly profit/interest payout for living expenses.
6. Lakhopoti & Kotipoti Savings Schemes: Structured installment schemes targeted to reach 1 Lakh or 1 Crore Taka target upon maturity.

SECTION 5: ACCOUNT OPENING REQUIREMENTS & REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

1. INDIVIDUAL SAVINGS / CURRENT ACCOUNT:
- Duly filled and signed Account Opening Form (or online via e-KYC).
- 2 passport-size photographs of the applicant.
- Copy of National ID Card (NID), Smart Card, Valid Passport, or Online Birth Registration Certificate.
- Proof of Address: Copy of recent utility bill (Electricity, Gas, Water) / Rent receipt.
- e-TIN Certificate (if available).
- Nominee Information: 1 passport-size photo and NID/Birth Certificate copy of the nominee (signed by applicant).
- Initial opening deposit amount.

2. STUDENT ACCOUNT (PARUA / UCCHASHA / STUDENT):
- Student NID or Birth Certificate.
- Valid Student ID Card or Certificate from educational institution / fee receipt.
- 2 passport-size photos of the student.
- Guardian's NID and 1 passport-size photo (if student is a minor under 18).

3. PROBASHI (NRB) ACCOUNT:
- Valid Bangladeshi Passport with valid visa / residence permit (Akama/Work Permit).
- Proof of employment/income abroad (Salary certificate / employment contract / pay slip).
- 2 passport-size photos of the applicant.
- Nominee photo and NID/Passport (residing in Bangladesh to operate as authorized nominee).

4. BUSINESS / CURRENT ACCOUNT (PROPRIETORSHIP / PARTNERSHIP / COMPANY):
- Valid Trade License.
- e-TIN of the business and proprietor.
- NID and photographs of Proprietor / Partners / Managing Director & Directors.
- Partnership Deed (for Partnership firm).
- Memorandum & Articles of Association + Certificate of Incorporation + Board Resolution (for Limited Companies).
